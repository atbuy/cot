# cot

`cot` is a screenshot tool for Wayland and X11. It freezes your screen the moment you press the keybind, then lets you select, annotate and redact on that frozen image.

Because the picture is taken before any window of its own appears, hover states, open menus and tooltips stay in the screenshot.

!!! tip "New here?"

    Start with [Getting started](tutorial/getting-started.md). It takes you from install to your first screenshot.

## What it does

- **Keeps what you were seeing.** The screen is captured first, so a hovered button is still hovered.
- **Works across monitors.** Different resolutions and rotated monitors are handled, and a selection can span them.
- **Selects quickly.** Drag a region, click a window, or Ctrl+click a whole monitor, then move or resize the result.
- **Annotates.** Arrows, rectangles, freehand, highlighter and text, with undo and redo.
- **Redacts.** Black bars, blur and pixelate, written directly into the final image.
- **Stays local.** Screenshots go to your clipboard or a folder you choose, and nothing is sent anywhere.
